Resources for families and caregivers

Home care rarely arrives on its own. Most families are also sorting out Medicaid, transportation, meals, and paperwork at the same time. These are the programs, agencies, and forms we point people to most often, gathered in one place so you are not hunting for them.

Need help right now?

Call us at (313) 917-7979. We will tell you which program fits, verify your insurance at no cost, and help you fill out the forms. You do not have to be a client to ask.

To report suspected abuse, neglect, or exploitation of a vulnerable adult, call Michigan Adult Protective Services at 855-444-3911, 24 hours a day. In an emergency, call 911.

01

State of Michigan

MDHHS and state programs

The Michigan Department of Health and Human Services runs Medicaid, the Home Help program that pays for most of the care we provide, and the adult services that sit alongside it.

Michigan Dept. of Health & Human Services (MDHHS)

The state department behind Medicaid, Home Help, food assistance, and adult protective services. The starting point for almost every program on this page.

MDHHS Adults & Seniors

Program pages for older adults and adults with disabilities, including in-home services and placement options.

Michigan Medicaid

Eligibility rules, health plan choices, and what Medicaid covers in Michigan.

MI Bridges

Apply online for Medicaid, food assistance, cash assistance, and more, and check your case status.

MDHHS Forms & Publications

The official, always-current library of adult services forms. Use this if you need a version newer than the copies we host.

MDHHS Hotlines

Every MDHHS phone line in one place, including the 24-hour Adult Protective Services reporting number.

02

Families & patients

Support beyond the care visit

Meals, rides, benefits counselling, dementia support, and someone to call when something is not right. All of these are free or low cost, and none of them require you to be our client.

Adult Protective Services

If you suspect a vulnerable adult is being abused, neglected, or financially exploited, report it. Available 24 hours a day at 855-444-3911.

MDHHS Aging & Community Living Services

Statewide programs for older adults and adults with disabilities, including nutrition, caregiver support, and the network of Area Agencies on Aging.

Detroit Area Agency on Aging

Meals, transportation, caregiver support, and benefits counselling for Wayne County, Detroit, Highland Park, Hamtramck, and the Grosse Pointes.

MMAP (Medicare/Medicaid Assistance Program)

Free, unbiased one-on-one counselling on Medicare and Medicaid decisions. Not a sales call.

Michigan 2-1-1

Dial 2-1-1 for local help with rent, utilities, food, transportation, and healthcare anywhere in the state.

Michigan Long Term Care Ombudsman

Free advocates who resolve complaints about care in nursing homes, adult foster care, and homes for the aged.

Alzheimer's Association of Michigan

Dementia caregiver education, local support groups, and a 24/7 helpline at 800-272-3900.

Eldercare Locator

Enter any Michigan ZIP code to find the Area Agency on Aging and local services for that community. Run by the U.S. Administration for Community Living.

Family Caregiver Alliance

Practical guides on burnout, difficult conversations, legal planning, and caring for yourself while caring for someone else.
