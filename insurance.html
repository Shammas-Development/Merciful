Questions

Coverage questions we hear a lot

Do you accept my insurance?

We accept all insurance companies that operate in the State of Michigan, including Medicaid and waiver programs. Call us with your plan name and we will confirm your specific benefits at no cost.

What if I do not have insurance?

We offer straightforward private-pay hourly rates with no long-term contract. We will also help you find out whether you qualify for Medicaid or a waiver program that would cover the care.

How many hours will be covered?

That depends on your plan and your assessed level of need. After the in-home assessment we submit the documentation and tell you exactly how many authorized hours you have.

Can you pay a caregiver I already have?

Often, yes. If someone is already caring for you in your home without being paid, we can hire them so they are paid for the services they provide. Family members, friends, and neighbors are all welcome to apply.

Do you serve my county?

We service all 83 counties in the State of Michigan. Wherever your loved one lives, call us and we will put care in place.

Let us check your coverage

One phone call and we will verify your benefits, explain your options, and tell you exactly where you stand. No cost, no obligation.
